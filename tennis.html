The Four Grand Slams

  1. Australian Open
    • Played on hard court
    • Held in Melbourne, Australia
    • The first Grand Slam of the year
  2. French Open
    • Played on clay
    • The slowest surface in tennis
    • Rafael Nadal won it 14 times
  3. Wimbledon
    • Played on grass
    • The oldest tournament in tennis
    • Players must wear all white
  4. US Open
    • Played on hard court
    • Held in New York City
    • The final Grand Slam of the year
